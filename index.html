About me

Understanding people through science and colour

I'm a psychology student drawn to the questions behind human behavior: why we think, feel and act the way we do. I enjoy reading psychological research and learning how careful methods turn curiosity into understanding.

Painting and visual design are the other half of who I am. Working with colour, composition and form helps me observe more closely and express what words can't always hold.
